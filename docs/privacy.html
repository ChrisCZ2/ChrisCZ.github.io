Privacy Policy

Last updated August 22, 2026. Trackz is a time-tracking app. This page explains what we collect and why. Use this URL when you publish Trackz as a public Google or GitHub app.

What Trackz collects

When you create an account we store your name, email address, and a hashed password. If you sign in with Google or GitHub, we store the name, email, avatar, and provider account id those services send back. We also store the projects, clients, teams, and time entries you create.

How we use it

We use this data only to run your Trackz workspace: keep you signed in, show your dashboard, and save your timers. We do not sell personal data. We do not use your Google or GitHub account for anything except sign-in.

Cookies

Trackz sets one httpOnly session cookie (trackz.sid) so you stay logged in. It is not used for advertising.

Where it is stored

On GitHub Pages, Trackz runs in your browser and stores workspace data in local storage on this device. On a self-hosted or local install, workspace data is encrypted on disk. Passwords are stored as irreversible hashes, not plain text. Secrets such as .env and the encrypted database are never part of the public project files. If you deploy Trackz, keep SESSION_SECRET and DATA_ENCRYPTION_KEY only in the host’s private environment variables.

Your choices

You can log out at any time. To delete an account, contact the operator of the Trackz instance you use. You can also revoke Trackz from Google account connections or GitHub application settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the operator of your Trackz site, or to the support email you list on the Google Auth Platform branding screen.
